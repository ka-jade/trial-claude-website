Diet and Lifestyle Therapies

Therapeutic diet and lifestyle interventions can help prevent and manage many chronic conditions—and, in selected cases, achieve remission. Clinical trials and other research help identify which dietary approaches, physical activity strategies, sleep interventions, and related behaviors improve health outcomes, and where the evidence remains uncertain.
