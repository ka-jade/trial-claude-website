Research area

Supplements

Vitamins, minerals, botanicals, and other dietary supplements can exert meaningful physiological effects, but their clinical benefits vary by compound, indication, dose, and formulation. Evidence from randomized controlled trials, when available, and other primary studies is evaluated alongside mechanistic and safety research to distinguish established benefits from preliminary findings and identify potential risks.
